\section{Support for Community Science} \label{sec:support}

The overall model for supporting the community to do science with Rubin data products, services, and tools was described for Data Preview 1 in \cite{RTN-095}.
For the release of Prompt products, we developed documentation at \url{https://prompt-products.lsst.io/} and tutorials based on Prompt-processed data for the Notebook, Portal, and API aspects of the Rubin Science Platform.
A series of virtual alerts and broker tutorials were held January to April 2026 as part of the Rubin Science Assemblies, and recordings are available\footnote{\url{https://rubinobservatory.org/for-scientists/events-deadlines/events-rsa}}.

The Rubin Community Forum\footnote{\url{https://community.lsst.org/}} is the primary venue for user support and issue resolution (as a modern, open-source helpdesk).
All are encouraged to post their Rubin-related questions there, and Rubin staff monitor the Support category.
Several of the broker teams (ALeRCE, Fink, and Lasair, at the time of publication) are using sub-categories in the Forum to Support their own users, and Rubin staff support them in this effort.
